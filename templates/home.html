Welcome to My Planner

This app helps you manage your time, study, and focus using:

  • Weekly Planner – schedule classes, homework, and events.
  • Spotify – play focus or study playlists while you work.
  • Canvas – keep an eye on assignments and due dates.
  • Pomodoro Timer – stay productive with timed focus sessions.

How to navigate

  1. Click Planner in the top-right nav bar, or use the button below to open your weekly planner view.
  2. Use the Spotify tab to open the music integration page.
  3. Use the Canvas tab to see assignments and course links.
  4. Use the Pomodoro Timer tab to start focused work sessions.
Go to Planner

Tips for using this planner

  • Block off your class times first in the planner.
  • Add study blocks before big exams or project deadlines.
  • Use the Pomodoro Timer for intense focus sessions.
  • Keep Spotify open with a chill or lo-fi playlist while you work.
  • Check Canvas at least once a day for new assignments.
